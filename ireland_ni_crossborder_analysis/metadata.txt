about=Ireland–Northern Ireland Cross-Border Analysis is a QGIS plugin for nearest-feature analysis across the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland. Key features include support for EPA Ireland, DAERA/NIEA Open Data, Environment Agency and Natural England datasets; automatic CRS selection between Irish Grid, British National Grid and UTM zones; nearest feature distance and direction calculation; CSV export; and map export. The plugin is designed for environmental assessment, planning, ecology, renewable energy, infrastructure and cross-border spatial analysis projects.
